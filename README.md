Entry for the [make.fun bounty "What the Elevator Would Say"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-eager-stork-81-9647-what-the-elevator-would-say-90844958).

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only) writes one line of at most 120 characters to `/output/entry.txt`: the funniest thing a talking elevator would say, in its own voice — a lift that remembers every passenger's ex, every floor they pretend not to need, and never judges out loud. It just dings.

> I know which floor your ex lives on, and I know you still press it "by accident." I never judge. I just ding.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
